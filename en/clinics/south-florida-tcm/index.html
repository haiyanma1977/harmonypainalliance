HPA Partner Clinic

Clinical Focus

What this clinic primarily works with:

  • Sciatica and chronic neck, shoulder, and back pain
  • Post-stroke rehabilitation and neurological recovery
  • Knee pain, arthritis, and mobility problems

Care Team

Huayu (Lucy) Brooks, AP

Florida Licensed Acupuncture Physician

Lucy Brooks is a Florida Licensed Acupuncture Physician with a Doctor of Acupuncture and Oriental Medicine (DAOM) degree and approximately 20 years of experience in Tui Na and therapeutic bodywork. Her approach combines acupuncture with Traditional Chinese Medicine therapies according to each patient's individual condition.

Services

  • Acupuncture
  • Tui Na (Chinese medical massage)
  • Chinese herbal medicine
  • Cupping
  • Ear seed therapy
  • Other Traditional Chinese Medicine therapies

When appropriate, acupuncture is combined with Tui Na and other TCM modalities rather than relying on a single treatment method.

Available services may vary by patient and clinical indication.

Additional Clinic Information

Acupuncture is intended to complement, not replace, care from your physician and rehabilitation team.
