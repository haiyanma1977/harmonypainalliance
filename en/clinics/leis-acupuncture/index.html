Clinical Focus

Neurological Rehabilitation

Lei's Acupuncture focuses on patients with neurological conditions and longer-term rehabilitation needs. Common areas of care include:

  • Post-stroke rehabilitation and recovery support
  • Facial paralysis and Bell's palsy
  • Neuropathy and nerve-related symptoms
  • Tinnitus
  • Migraine and chronic headache

Chronic & Complex Pain

Lei's Acupuncture also focuses on patients experiencing chronic, recurring, or complex pain. Common areas of care include:

  • Chronic neck and shoulder pain
  • Low back pain and sciatica
  • Neuropathic and nerve-related pain
  • Persistent musculoskeletal pain
  • Chronic or recurring pain involving multiple factors

The Leisacupuncture Approach

Leisacupuncture (磊氏针灸) represents the clinical approach used at Lei's Acupuncture. Care is individualized around each patient's overall condition and clinical presentation, rather than relying on the same routine for every patient or focusing only on the location of a symptom.

Is This Clinic a Good Fit for You?

Lei's Acupuncture may be a good fit if you are:

  • Seeking supportive acupuncture care during neurological rehabilitation
  • Managing persistent, chronic, or complex pain
  • Looking for an individualized acupuncture treatment plan based on your condition and clinical assessment

What to Expect

Your First Visit

A first visit generally includes:

  • Review of medical and symptom history
  • Discussion of current concerns and functional limitations
  • Review of medications and relevant rehabilitation care
  • Individualized acupuncture assessment
  • Discussion of the treatment plan
  • Recommended treatment frequency when appropriate

Individualized Care

Treatment is based on the patient's primary concern, medical and symptom history, duration and severity of symptoms, stage of recovery, functional limitations, previous treatment response, and overall clinical assessment. For neurological rehabilitation and long-standing pain, care may involve a series of treatments with periodic reassessment.

Acupuncture is intended to complement, not replace, care from your physician and rehabilitation team.

Services

Depending on the patient's condition and clinical assessment, services may include:

  • Acupuncture
  • Electroacupuncture
  • Traditional Chinese Medicine
  • Chinese Herbal Medicine
  • Cupping

Available services may vary by patient and clinical indication.
