About the Clinic

First, I have patients fill out a detailed online intake form before they come in for their first visit...this way I am prepared with follow-up questions and have an idea of how the session will go. I also analyze any medications and/or supplements they are on, for any possible side-effects, interactions or nutrient depletions, which may be contributing or causing their health concerns. I can also review any bloodwork or imaging reports they have, and tie that information into my diagnosis.

I will review their case from both a TCM and an integrative medicine lens and talk to them about diet, lifestyle modifications and nutritional supplements, as well as acupuncture, electro-acupuncture, cupping, gua sha and Chinese herbal medicine. And I end up giving the patient all of this information in a comprehensive Report of Findings at their first or second visit.

Dawn Potter has been a licensed acupuncture physician in Florida since 2004.
