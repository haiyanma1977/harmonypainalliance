About the Clinic

Health Pointe Jacksonville is an acupuncture and integrative wellness clinic with a strong focus on women’s health, reproductive medicine, pelvic health, pregnancy and complex pain conditions.

What sets our practice apart is the depth of our focus and the way we integrate acupuncture into a patient’s larger medical care. We frequently work alongside reproductive endocrinologists, OB/GYNs, pelvic floor therapists and other healthcare providers to support patients through fertility treatment, IVF/IUI/FET cycles, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, hormonal transitions and chronic pelvic pain.

Our treatments are highly individualized and may include traditional acupuncture, electroacupuncture, trigger point acupuncture, dry needling techniques, cupping and gua sha.

Our work centers especially on subfertility, fertility treatment support, pelvic floor dysfunction, endometriosis-related pain, migraine and women’s health conditions. Our goal is to combine clinical experience, thoughtful assessment and collaborative care in a warm, patient-centered environment where patients feel heard, supported and actively involved in their care.

Health Pointe Jacksonville has served the Jacksonville community since 2008.
