Now Accepting Early Partner Clinics

Early partner clinics may be considered for limited Founding Partner Clinic recognition after review and an initial partnership period.

HPA Partner Clinic

After an initial review, clinics join the network as Partner Clinics.

  • Clinic profile and multilingual listing
  • Patient referrals matched to clinical focus
  • Reviewed personally by HPA's founder
  • Access to optional training and platform services

Founding Partner Clinic Recognition

Partner Clinics that show strong clinical focus and active participation during HPA's launch phase may be invited to become Founding Partner Clinics.

  • Priority platform placement
  • Priority consideration for patient referrals
  • A voice in the Alliance's early development
  • Founding Partner recognition
  • Limited availability; granted by invitation
