Sobre la clínica (en inglés)

Health Pointe Jacksonville is an acupuncture and integrative wellness clinic with a strong focus on women’s health, reproductive medicine, pelvic health, pregnancy and complex pain conditions.

What sets our practice apart is the depth of our focus and the way we integrate acupuncture into a patient’s larger medical care. We frequently work alongside reproductive endocrinologists, OB/GYNs, pelvic floor therapists and other healthcare providers to support patients through fertility treatment, IVF/IUI/FET cycles, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, hormonal transitions and chronic pelvic pain.

Our treatments are highly individualized and may include traditional acupuncture, electroacupuncture, trigger point acupuncture, dry needling techniques, cupping and gua sha.

We are especially known for our work with subfertility, fertility treatment support, pelvic floor dysfunction, endometriosis-related pain, migraine and women’s health conditions. Our goal is to combine clinical experience, thoughtful assessment and collaborative care in a warm, patient-centered environment where patients feel heard, supported and actively involved in their care.

Health Pointe Jacksonville has served the Jacksonville community since 2008.

Acerca de Harmony Pain Alliance

Harmony Pain Alliance ayuda a los pacientes a encontrar clínicas independientes de acupuntura y Medicina Tradicional China según su ubicación, enfoque clínico y trayectoria profesional.

Durante la fase de lanzamiento de HPA, cada clínica de la plataforma es revisada personalmente por nuestra fundadora antes de unirse.

Health Pointe Jacksonville Acupuncture + Wellness Clinic es una clínica socia de Harmony Pain Alliance en Jacksonville, Florida.
